[b]Fixes and clean-up for the Genesis Mod Pack and its Combat Extended edition.[/b]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Fixes small conflicts between mods in the pack.
[*]Makes the fourth and fifth mouse buttons work again as Letter Stack Cleaner's key on RimWorld 1.7.
[*]Hides the "Need research bench" alert until you have unlocked a research bench.
[*]Hides a few known, harmless startup errors from other mods while their authors work on fixes, so the errors you do see are ones that matter.
[*]Each hidden error is noted in the log as an info line instead, and every other error is left untouched.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Made for the Genesis Mod Pack and its Combat Extended edition, and included in both. Safe to add to an existing save.
